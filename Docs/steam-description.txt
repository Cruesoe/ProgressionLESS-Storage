[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3292746186]Progression: Storage[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Integrates storage mods into vanilla progression, so each storage building unlocks in its proper tech era.
[*]Rebalances storage capacity and descriptions across the supported storage mods.
[*]Hides duplicate storage buildings when several storage mods overlap.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Storage adds up to [b]22 research projects[/b] depending on your storage mods. This fork cuts that to [b]at most 4[/b], one per era:
[list]
[*][b]Basic storage[/b] (Neolithic): shelves, pallets, racks, baskets, sacks, bales and other simple storage.
[*][b]Crates and chests[/b] (Medieval): crates, barrels, chests, strongboxes and meat hooks.
[*][b]Display pod[/b] (Spacer)
[*][b]Manipulator beam emitter[/b] (Ultra)
[/list]
Industrial storage (drums, safes, fridges) uses the vanilla research it already builds on: Machining and Air conditioning.

Every building stays in the same era, and still needs any other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Medieval Overhaul, plus its adaptive storage addon
[*]Reel's Expanded Storage
[*]Neat Storage, Neat Storage Fridge and SBZ Bookcase
[*]Gravship Storage and Cargo Holds
[*]Adaptive Primitive Storage
[*]Vanilla Furniture Expanded, VFE Art, VFE Spacer, VFE Mechanoids, VFE Medieval 2 and VE Recycling
[*]Ponpeco Furnitures Sci-Fi (with ReBuild: Doors and Corners)
[*]Manipulator Beam Emitter
[*]Vending Machines, Pass It Through the Window and Alpha Books
[*]Odyssey
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Storage for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
